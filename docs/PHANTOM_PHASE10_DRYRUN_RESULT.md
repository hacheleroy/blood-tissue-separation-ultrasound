# Phase-10 physical handoff: input and packet dry-run

26 October 2026. This is an **engineering fixture**, not a phantom capture.
It supplements the [phase-10 physical bridge](PHANTOM_PHASE10_BRIDGE.md)
and its [incomplete measured-acquisition plan](../runs/phantom_phase10_plan/).
The frozen phase-10 geometry-aware port and reserve archive are unchanged.
The [input-only preflight](../scripts/phantom_phase10_preflight.py) checks the
plan and, once measured files exist, validates an entire capture inventory
before filtering. The [dry-run](../scripts/phantom_phase10_dryrun.py) generates
one explicitly simulated static/slow-flow matched triplet and exercises all
twelve score windows. Its [compact report](../runs/phantom_phase10_dryrun/report.json)
contains source hashes, support and selector audits, packet output hashes,
paired metric plumbing and eight rejection checks. The simulated IQ, dummy
RF bytes and reference fields stay outside the repository.

## Preflight scope

The repository's original scaffold reproduces **2 sessions × 3 motion
conditions × 2 speeds × 5 repeats × 3 matched captures = 180 slots**. The
new `plan` command checks every cell, capture name and path, reserve and port
source identity, 32×16 wide IQ, 8-row score core and the twelve window starts
`0, 16, …, 176`. It returns `physical_eligible: false` on the intentionally
unfilled card. The strict `preflight` command rejects that card. On a future
filled card, it also requires:

- Three **measured** true transmit views in the order 0°, −5°, +5°, within
  0.5° each, valid receive angles, transmit offsets and same-view PRF;
- A hashed preacquisition structural centerline/lumen, the derived 5/5.5 mm
  wide/core guards, disjoint fit/check pixels and separately scored flow and
  tissue masks with a one-pixel guard;
- Complex unfiltered 256-frame wide IQ plus a **prior**, stationary,
  pump-off 64-frame calibration for every capture, uniform same-view clocks,
  16-frame phase-10 calibration support and original raw-RF/beamformer hashes;
- Matched tissue-only, seeded pump-off and pump-on captures, unique RF/IQ
  across controls, independent optical/stage motion fields with timestamps
  and uncertainty, and independently measured flow rate within ±20% of
  the 3 or 20 mm/s target;
- A complete fresh-remount session, card timestamp before capture and fixed
  anatomy/geometry across sessions.

The preflight checks file structure and recorded values. It cannot
authenticate that a claimed scanner, camera or flowmeter measurement really
occurred; retain the independently dated laboratory card and raw records.
Its successful return would mean **analysis input eligibility**, not a
filter-performance result. Real wide IQ on a different grid or without
three true transmit views requires a new, frozen preacquisition protocol.

## Simulated end-to-end fixture

The fixture uses a 32×16 grid, a structural 60° centerline, 193 wide and
40 core tissue pixels (128/65 disjoint wide fit/check), a 12-pixel flow
ROI and an independent eight-pixel tissue score ROI. Three distinct
synthetic captures have finite complex IQ, 64 earlier calibration frames,
256 evaluation frames, explicit same-view timestamps and separate dummy
RF/reference files. Their first 16 calibration frames pass the frozen
angular support check. It supplies a synthetic 0.02-cycle/frame lumen
oscillation and no tissue motion. These arrays are **generated by the dry-run
script** and are not Resolve Stroke or laboratory observations.

Every capture was processed in the prescribed twelve overlapping
80-frame windows; only each window's last 16 frames were assembled, giving
the exact contiguous score interval `[64,256)`. The data-only selector
chose **hybrid in all 36 windows**. For the selected Axis 1 cut-1 path,
maximum roundtrip relative error across all windows was
**1.43 × 10⁻¹⁶**. The 192-frame slow Doppler band contained 18 FFT bins,
and the constructed on-minus-off input excess was identifiable. As a
*software arithmetic check*, the synthetic paired response ratio was
**0.611** for selected Axis 1 versus **0.611** for the no-motion Axis 1
control; selected tissue-only output/input power was
**4.43 × 10⁻⁵**. The response ratio is below the proposed 0.8 physical
threshold even in this fixture. No threshold, filter parameter or fixture
scene is adjusted from that result. One synthetic triplet cannot run the
six-cell, whole-triplet physical gate.

Eight negative checks rejected a missing matched control, an angle outside
tolerance, missing independent reference, undocumented prior calibration,
missing flowmeter, calibration after evaluation, a tissue score mask placed
inside the lumen, and the simulated card submitted as measured physical
input. The fixture reports `physical_eligible: false` regardless of its
software checks.

```bash
python scripts/phantom_phase10_preflight.py plan \

  --output /path/to/new/fixture_report.json
```

The remaining external inputs are the measured scanner/beamformer and
three-view timing, structural centerline/wall/PSF, optical transform and
motion fields, pump/flowmeter/PIV calibration, matched bubble-free captures,
and independently dated development and held-out sessions. The full
180-capture physical preflight has **not** passed and no physical response
has been measured.
